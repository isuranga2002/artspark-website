About ArtSpark

Founded in 2024 by graduates, for students who cannot afford to be late.

ArtSpark is a study-consulting desk staffed only by graduates of Sri Lankan government universities. Fixed delivery times, free similarity and AI reports, and complete confidentiality — on every order.

Since2024
Why we started

The market had two failures. We had lived through both as students.

Cheap work that failed the similarity check, or careful work that arrived the day after the deadline. Either way the student lost a grade — sometimes a whole module.

ArtSpark was built to fix both at once. Every delivery date is fixed in writing, every file is read by a second person, and every order comes with the similarity and AI-detection reports, so quality can be checked rather than promised.

The panel

Every writer is a graduate of a Sri Lankan government university.

Writers join the panel for the faculty they studied in, and they write only inside it. Each order is matched on subject and level, a coordinator stays with it from quote to delivery, and an editor reads everything before it leaves us.

We publish the faculties rather than the names — the same confidentiality we give you, we give them.

How we work

Four rules we do not bend.

Plagiarism free

Written from source material for your brief, checked digitally and read by a proofreader before it leaves us. Similarity stays low, and the report proves it.

Always on time

The delivery date and time are fixed in your quote. We turn work down rather than miss a deadline — that is the whole point of choosing us.

Free Turnitin & AI report

Every delivery includes a similarity report and an AI-detection report. You never take our word for it, and you never pay extra for the proof.

100% confidential

Your name, university and documents are never shown to anyone. Writers see the brief, not you, and files are deleted on request.

Consulting, not shortcuts

We want you to understand the work — not just receive it.

Our material is built to learn from: a clear structure, sources you can follow up, explained analysis, and coaching for the parts you must present or defend yourself. Ask why a framework was chosen or what a regression result means, and you will get a real answer.

We encourage every student to use our work in line with their university's academic-integrity policy. Read our full position in our privacy & terms.
