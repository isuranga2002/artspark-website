How it works

Six steps. Only two of them are yours.

  1. Send the brief

    Share the question, rubric, word count, deadline and referencing style. A photo of the handout is fine.

  2. Free quote in 30 min

    One fixed price and a delivery date and time — never a range. If we cannot make it, we say so.

  3. Confirm the order

    Approve the price and delivery time on WhatsApp and your file is booked into the schedule.

  4. A matched writer starts

    Your work goes to a graduate of that faculty, not whoever is free. Long projects get progress notes.

  5. Delivery with proof

    The document, the similarity report and the AI-detection report arrive together, on the agreed date.

  6. Revise until it fits

    Free revisions for seven days against the original brief — including changes your lecturer asks for.

What is ArtSpark?

ArtSpark is a Sri Lankan study-consulting service for university and professional students.

Founded in 2024, ArtSpark works with students across Sri Lanka and with Sri Lankan students studying abroad. It provides assignment writing, thesis and dissertation support, research and data analysis, journal articles, case studies, presentations, academic coaching, and editing and proofreading — for MBA, MSc, BSc, BA, HND, SLIM, CIM, PQHRM, NVQ, diploma and PhD level work.

Every writer is a graduate of a Sri Lankan government university. Every delivery includes a Turnitin similarity report and an AI-detection report at no extra charge, work is quoted with a fixed price and delivery time, and revisions are free for seven days. How much does an order cost?

Price depends on the level, the word count, the deadline and how technical the work is. Send your brief and you will receive one fixed price within 30 minutes — no hidden fees and no obligation to accept.

Can you deliver in 24 hours?

Often, yes — especially for shorter work. When you ask for a quote we tell you honestly what is possible in your time frame. We would rather decline a job than miss a deadline.

Do I really get the Turnitin and AI-detection reports?

Yes. Every delivery includes a Turnitin similarity report and an AI-detection report at no extra charge. We keep plagiarism and AI-generated text below 10%, and the reports let you confirm it yourself.

Will my details be kept confidential?

Always. Your name, university and documents are never shared or published. Writers see the brief, not your identity, and your files are deleted on request once the order is complete.
